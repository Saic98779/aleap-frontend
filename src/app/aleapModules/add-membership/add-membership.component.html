RULES AND REGULATIONS

I. NAME

The Name of the Association is "Association of Lady Entrepreneurs of India" (ALEAP-India).

II. OFFICE

The Office of "Association of Lady Entrepreneurs of India" is located at 8-2-120/86/9/A/30, Road No.2, Banjara Hills, Behind LUCID Diagnostics, Hyderabad-500034.

III. OBJECTIVES

  1. To Develop entrepreneurial skills in women and assist them in starting their own industry.
  2. To arrange and organize training programmes, Seminars and Conferences in the field to taxation, finance, exports and management for imparting skills/education.
  3. To help the Members in their role towards the community and nation at large in activities of social welfare.
  4. To collaborate and establish effective liaison and coordination between the government industry and commerce in all activities relating to the above subjects.
  5. To send delegations abroad and to receive delegations from foreign countries.
  6. Any activity incidental and related to the above subjects.

IV. MEMBERSHIP

  1. There shall be the following membership:
    1. Individual membership shall be open to ladies engaged in trade, commerce or any other procession or industry.
    2. Life Membership will be open to individuals, Companies, Corporations and Associations.
    3. Institutional Membership shall be open to the companies, Corporate Bodies, Firm or Partnership and other similar activity owned or managed by ladies.
    4. Association Membership shall be open to the ladies Organizations, Local Organizations, Local Chamber of commerce, Member bodies whose objectives are similar to those of the association.
  2. All applications for membership must be proposed and seconded by existing members of the "Association of Lady Entrepreneurs of India" (ALEAP-India).
  3. The Managing Committee of the Association shall be the sole judge as to the eligibility of an applicant for membership.
  4. The cost of the applicant is Rs.10/-.
